# The site

A projection of this repository and of [thevzion/standard](https://github.com/thevzion/standard):
generated from them, never written apart. One page ([decision](../decisions/one-page.md)): the
promise with the "I want" gesture, the toy that shows it happen, three facts with their status, and
the way to the two repositories. Manifesto, Standard and Progress stay as drafts, out of the links.

On the home page, motions people feel; the diagrams (the meta-model, the stack, the trees) go to the
other pages ([decision](../decisions/motion-on-the-home-page.md)). The home page shows the shift
([decision](../decisions/landing-shows-the-shift.md)) and the roles in the pure state
([decision](../decisions/roles-in-the-pure-state.md)).

**Is it done?** No. Nothing is published. The home page motions are to be redone, candidate
principles: real content, one object that persists and changes, an actor for each gesture, few
elements, a calm camera, and two or three beats tested in motion first.
