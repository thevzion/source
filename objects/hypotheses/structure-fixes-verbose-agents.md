# Structure fixes verbose agents

Verbosity comes from what is missing: an agent that doesn't know what the person wants covers
every case. Compressing its output treats the format; explicit objects treat the cause.

## How we'll know

- **Anchoring**: the share of what is produced that traces back to human intent, directly or
  through the space's objects.
- **Leverage**: the volume produced per line of human intent. High leverage with low anchoring is
  the generic document.

See [the measures](../../standard/measures.md).
