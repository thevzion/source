# It carries over to people

What works for an agent works for a person too: a partner or a teammate reads the same objects,
through shared models and pointers, and nothing private leaves by default.

## How we'll know

- A person's goal serves the organization's goal, a task passes to a partner, and the person's
  capital stays invisible to the others.
- Another person, like another agent, picks up where things were.
