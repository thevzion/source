# Agents hold roles reliably, under mandates

Every role can be held by anyone or anything. For an agent to hold one, it works under a mandate:
what it may do, and where it must stop. The bet is that, with the role and the mandate written,
an agent holds the role well enough that the person stops checking each step.

## How we'll know

- **Accuracy** of what agents inferred while holding a role, and how often a mandate was crossed.
- **Gates**: approval requests per session go down as mandates are written.
- The same role, under the same mandate, held by another agent gives the same kind of result.

See [the measures](https://github.com/thevzion/standard/blob/main/standard/measures.md).
