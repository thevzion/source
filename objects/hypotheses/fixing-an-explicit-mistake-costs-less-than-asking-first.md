# Fixing an explicit mistake costs less than asking first

In a conversation, you don't know what the agent understood. Written as objects, a mistake is
visible, and you point at exactly what is wrong: an object, a type, a link. Explicit mistakes are
cheap.

## How we'll know

- **Accuracy**: the share of what was inferred that is later corrected, and the final accuracy of
  the objects, when the agent writes first versus when it asks first.

See [the measures](https://github.com/thevzion/standard/blob/main/standard/measures.md).
