# Your intent is the source, intelligence runs it

What you want, decide and know, made explicit, is the source; any intelligence runs it, and the
loop rewrites the source with what each turn learns. Candidate, not yet on the page.

## How we'll know

- On a first run, raw intents become typed objects, linked by `serves`, that an agent acts on.
- They land in the model's types, consistently across agents.
- The second turn starts from what the first one left.
