# You stop babysitting your agent

Don't watch the agent work. Read what it wrote. The agent commits, marks what it inferred, and you
review when you choose; you stop approving each step.

## How we'll know

- **Gates**: approval requests per session, and the gaps between the written agreement and real
  access.

See [the measures](../../standard/measures.md).
