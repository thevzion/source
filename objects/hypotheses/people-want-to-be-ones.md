# People want to be ones

The one of an organization of one is the person who wants it and answers for it. That anyone can
be one is the structure's promise; that people want to be is a separate bet. Some may prefer to
hold roles in someone else's organization.

## How we'll know

- People who start an organization of one keep writing what they want in it, weeks later.
- They answer for it themselves rather than handing the wanting back.
