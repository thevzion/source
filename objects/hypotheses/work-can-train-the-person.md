# Work can train the person

The agent discerns for the person, and the person learns to discern by reviewing what it filed.
The forms of the explicit (a goal with how we'll know, a decision with its reason) are learned by
use.

## How we'll know

- **Capital signals**: a person's rephrasings, wrong placements and corrections, branch by branch,
  over weeks; private, with the person's consent, for that person alone.
- On the second turn, the person spots alone what was caught on the first.

See [the measures](https://github.com/thevzion/standard/blob/main/standard/measures.md).
