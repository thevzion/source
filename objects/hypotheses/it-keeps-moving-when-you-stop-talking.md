# It keeps moving when you stop talking

Routines ask their question on their own, on a trigger, under a mandate: file, advance, check,
refresh, watch, take stock, learn. The watch returns a question to the person, never a verdict.
Candidate, not yet on the page.

## How we'll know

- **Autonomy**: the share of work done by routines without a request, and the accuracy of what
  they inferred.
- What the person corrects, what a routine costs, and the balance.

See [the measures](https://github.com/thevzion/standard/blob/main/standard/measures.md).
