# Making it explicit adds no work

What the person stops doing (re-explaining, reviewing everything, going back and forth) outweighs
what they do in addition (reviewing what was inferred, upkeep). Part of the thesis, measured first.

## How we'll know

- **Balance** (net gain): time saved per intent, minus upkeep, minus setup. The hypothesis holds
  where it is positive.
- **Upkeep**: the share of changes that serve the structure rather than an intent.

Both are defined in [the measures](https://github.com/thevzion/standard/blob/main/standard/measures.md).
