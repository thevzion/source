# Switch models, keep everything

What accumulates lives in the space, not in an AI model or in a tool's memory. Change the AI model,
or the provider, and keep everything: when intelligence becomes interchangeable, explicit intent
is what remains. Candidate, not yet on the page.

## How we'll know

- An agent from another provider resumes the space and carries on, with nothing moved or
  rewritten.
