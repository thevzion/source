# Proposals across organizations don't drown in noise

When anyone can propose to anyone, the risk is noise: everyone proposes to everyone. The bet is
that a proposal written as an object, which says what it serves on the other side, can be sorted
and answered, and that what serves nothing there can be left without a cost.

## How we'll know

- **Waiting**: the number and age of open proposals stay readable as organizations connect.
- The share of proposals answered, accepted or declined, rather than left unseen.

See [the measures](https://github.com/thevzion/standard/blob/main/standard/measures.md).
