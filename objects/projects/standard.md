# The standard

The doctrine (how any intelligence behaves) and the structure (objects, links, changes, the trace,
truth, access), kept in [`standard/`](https://github.com/thevzion/standard/blob/main/standard/README.md). The standard library ships with
it, in [`models/`](https://github.com/thevzion/standard/blob/main/models/README.md).

One structure anyone can take, and many can test. A space of its own later; for now it lives in
this repository, next to TheVZion's space.

Version 0.1, candidate, untested.
