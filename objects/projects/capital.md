# The Capital

Makes the Capital model ([`models/capital/`](../../models/capital/model.md)): what a person can do
now that they couldn't before.

- Five branches: Clarity (want), Expression (say), Discernment (see), Judgment (choose), Learning
  (learn).
- A private reading of the loop: a person's signals (rephrasings, wrong placements, corrections),
  branch by branch, for that person alone.
- Capabilities, its type.

A person's capital never lives in a shared space.
