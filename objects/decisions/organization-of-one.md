# Organization of one

**Decision.** "Organization of one" is a canonical term, in English, on the page and in the
standard. It means an organization whose intent is that of a single human.

- The "one" counts who wants, not who works: agents act in it as much as needed, but one human
  wants and answers for it.
- "Human" rather than "person": a company is a person too, in law.
- You become one when your intent is explicit enough to be acted on. Everyone can be an
  organization now; not everyone is.

**Why.** It names the smallest organization the structure serves, and the center every space
starts from. "One human who wants. As many agents as it takes."

**Who answers.** The founder.
