# The review cost is accepted

**Decision.** Reviewing, correcting and upkeep stay a cost, accepted against the gains: provider
independence, and the yield of intelligence and of intent. The promise is a net gain, not a zero
cost, and the balance is measured.

**Why.** Any system in use needs upkeep to stay useful (Lehman's laws of software evolution), and
this cost is judged small against the gains.

**Who answers.** The founder.

**See.** [The measures](https://github.com/thevzion/standard/blob/main/standard/measures.md) (balance, upkeep),
[making it explicit adds no work](../hypotheses/adds-no-work.md).
