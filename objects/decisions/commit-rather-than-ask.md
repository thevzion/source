# Commit rather than ask

**Decision.** The agent commits rather than asking or waiting: it does the work and makes it
explicit, and the person corrects when they choose. Gates everywhere are a smell; a gate stays only
where a mistake can't be corrected precisely, or reaches someone else.

**Why.** Lived experience of watching an agent step by step.

**Who answers.** The founder.

**See.** [The doctrine](../../standard/doctrine.md), [access](../../standard/access.md),
[trial 10](../../tests/trials/10-commit-or-ask.md),
[you stop babysitting your agent](../hypotheses/you-stop-babysitting-your-agent.md).
