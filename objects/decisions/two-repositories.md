# The standard and this source live in two repositories

**Decision.** The product (the standard, the models, the setup prompt, the tests) lives in
[`thevzion/standard`](https://github.com/thevzion/standard). TheVZion's source lives here.

**Why.** A source is an instance, and the standard is its type: "we standardize the source". One
repository mixed the product and an instance, so someone taking the standard got TheVZion's goals,
and someone following TheVZion had to go through the standard. Decisions about the standard stay
here, because TheVZion decides and the standard is what it produces.

**Who answers.** The founder.
