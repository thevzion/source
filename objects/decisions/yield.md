# Yield

**Decision.** The word is "yield", not "maximize": what the system raises is the yield of intent
and of intelligence.

**Why.** The system doesn't make the agent smarter; it makes each unit of intelligence carry more
human intent. Yield says what comes out useful for what goes in.

**Who answers.** The founder.

**See.** [The measures](../../standard/measures.md) (yield, anchoring, leverage, fit).
