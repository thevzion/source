# The invariant

**Decision.** With or without an agent, the invariant is wanting, and answering for it. Not
judgment.

**Why.** Judgment, discernment, the choice of means, even taste: an intelligence can have them.
Nobody, agent or human, can decide for someone what they want, and that person answers for it.
"Intelligence can. Only people want, and answer for it."

**Replaces.** "You keep the judgment" (mission) and "People move up to judgment" (vision), which
lost the invariant. Now: "What you want stays yours" and "Intelligence runs what can be run. Only
people want."

**Who answers.** The founder.

**See.** [The doctrine](../../standard/doctrine.md), [mission](../mission.md),
[vision](../vision.md).
