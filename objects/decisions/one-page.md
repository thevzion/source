# The site is one page

**Decision.** The site is one page: the promise with the "I want" gesture, the toy that shows it
happen, three facts with their status, and the way to the two repositories. Manifesto, Standard and
Progress leave the links and the published site; they stay as drafts, kept and readable locally.

**Why.** The repositories now do what those pages did: [thevzion/standard](https://github.com/thevzion/standard)
is the standard in full, and this source is the state and the history. A short page that strikes
says more than four that explain.

**Who answers.** The founder.
