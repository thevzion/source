# A want, before its floor

**Decision.** What a person wants is what every object on the floors is, at its horizon. Each floor
is an end for the floor below and a means for the floor above; only the why is wanted for itself.
The standard adds no hierarchy of wants above the floors. A `want`, in the Loop model, is a want
with no horizon yet: placing it gives it a floor and a type. It can wait, be let go, or be got
before it is ever placed. Done is not got: only the person says got.

**Why.** `serves` stays the only chain of why. A want above the floors would add a second one, and
a sentence would have two valid places. It follows what the standard already says ("a goal is an
intent that lasts"; the floors) and the aim of a generic model.

**Who answers.** The founder. Inferred by the agent from what the founder said he wants; proposed
in thevzion/standard on a branch, `loop-want`, until he takes it.

[the invariant](the-invariant.md).
