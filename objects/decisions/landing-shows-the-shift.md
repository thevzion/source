# The landing shows the shift

**Decision.** The canonical view of the landing shows the paradigm shift, with and without an
agent: the same scheme three times, and only the middle changes. What a person wants, decides and
knows is already there, and so are the forms (goals, roadmaps, decisions, recipes).

| When | The middle | What comes out |
|---|---|---|
| before | the person, who fills the template, files, links and keeps it up to date, on top of the work | little: most of it stays implicit |
| today | an agent is added, the structure doesn't change | a thousand generic lines, all to review |
| now | the structure changed, and the agent does the work of making it explicit | small objects, anchored in what the person said; the person reviews what was inferred |

**Why.** What changed is who pays for making things explicit, not the forms. The drawing itself
stays candidate.

**Who answers.** The founder.
