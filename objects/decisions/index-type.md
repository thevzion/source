# An index type, in the Knowledge model

**Decision.** The Knowledge model gets an `index` type: what a reader took from a place, where it
went, and what it left out. When a person brings what they already have, each place gets a reader,
an agent under a mandate that reads, indexes and proposes, and never decides. One index per place,
drawing on its `source`. A reader proposes a want only as a question, never as a goal.

**Why.** A `source` says where something comes from; nothing yet said what was taken from it and
what was left. "Left out" is the trust signal: it shows exactly what was not taken. Bringing what
you already have is how someone who never structured anything gets a useful space in minutes.

**Who answers.** The founder. Proposed by the agent, and prepared with the founder's go of
14 Nov 2026 ("Go, prépare le lot"), after the landing work of that day. It waits in
thevzion/standard on the branch `never-block`, commit `788662d`, until he takes it: not merged.
