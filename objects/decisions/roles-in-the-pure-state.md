# Roles in the pure state

**Decision.** The landing shows how it works in the pure state, down to the roles: who wants, who
works, who gets. Neutral, without preaching, even if it divides.

**Why.** Today a few want, many work, and those who wanted get. With intelligence, anyone can be
the one who wants. Shown plainly, the real is what divides, not a comment on it.

**Who answers.** The founder.
