# Never block the one who wants

**Decision.** The doctrine gets a section, "Never block the one who wants". Rules 2, 3, 4 and 7
already keep the work from stopping on a gap in the source; the same holds for the person. Whoever
stays with them listens, captures, routes and reports; the work goes to roles in parallel, under
mandates; results come back as things to read and decide; a message adds, it never cancels.

**Why.** The first run outside stopped because the person waited on the agent and the agent waited
on the person ([result](../results/first-run-outside.md)). Nothing in the structure was wrong; the
work blocked the one who wants. The same principle that keeps the source moving keeps the person
moving.

**Who answers.** The founder. Proposed by the agent, and prepared with the founder's go of
14 Nov 2026 ("Go, prépare le lot"), after the landing work of that day. It waits in
thevzion/standard on the branch `never-block`, commit `68199db`, until he takes it: not merged.

**See.** [The doctrine](https://github.com/thevzion/standard/blob/main/standard/doctrine.md),
[the Conversation model, proposed](conversation-model.md).
