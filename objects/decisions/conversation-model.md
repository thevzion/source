# The Conversation model

**Decision.** The standard library gets a Conversation model, version 0.1, candidate: how what
someone says becomes work and comes back to them to decide. It has no types of its own; it takes
those of the Loop, Work and Agreements models. Three roles: the person, who wants, understands,
answers for it and validates; the chief of staff, who stays with them; and workers, each from a
brief. Every turn ends on what is now true, what is not, and one next step.

**Why.** The way a person and whoever stays with them talk while the work runs is a way of doing,
written in common: plain text any agent can follow, with no orchestration software. It is the
doctrine's "never block the one who wants", turn by turn, and it serves the same source for any
agent.

**Who answers.** The founder. Proposed by the agent, and prepared with the founder's go of
14 Nov 2026 ("Go, prépare le lot"), after the landing work of that day. It waits in
thevzion/standard on the branch `never-block`, commit `a458713`, until he takes it: not merged.
