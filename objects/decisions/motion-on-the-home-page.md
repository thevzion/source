# Motion on the home page

**Decision.** The home page shows motions, loops and scenes people understand and feel. The
architecture diagrams (the meta-model, the stack, the trees) go to the other pages.

**Why.** What we propose is simple enough that making it explicit was all it took; the home page
has to make that simplicity felt, not explain it.

**Who answers.** The founder.
