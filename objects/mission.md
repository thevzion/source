# Make your organization executable.

Seven questions. One folder. Then it compounds. ([decision](decisions/hero-line.md))

Write down what you're after, why, and how you'll know. Agents and people can act on it. What you
want stays yours.

The last sentence replaces "You keep the judgment", which lost the invariant: an intelligence can
judge, only people want ([decision](decisions/the-invariant.md)).

**For whom, first.** People who already work a lot with agents, because they live the
[problem](problem.md) every day. "Everyone needs it" stays an ambition, not a measure:
[goal 3](goals/3.md) says how we'll know.
