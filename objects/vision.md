# A world where nothing stands between what anyone wants, however big, and what they get.

Every role can be held by anyone or anything. Only wanting can't.

**The ambition.** Anyone can have an organization, contribute to anyone's, and live from what they
give. It has no measure yet: it becomes a goal the day it has one. How what you give comes back to
you is [still open](questions/what-you-give-comes-back.md). The Purpose and Goals models have no
ambition type, so it lives here, with the vision it aims at.

**Organizations of one, connected.** An organization is made of organizations of one, plugged into
it by `serves` links: a person's goal serves the organization's goal. Nothing is taken from the
person; what is shared is shared by pointer. Your work is shared, not you.

Organizations link to each other, and organizations of one link directly too. The same structure
at every size, up to the scale of the planet.

The previous vision, "A world where anyone, a person alone, an association, a small team, can rely
on the structure of an organization.", stays in history; its idea carries on in the ambition.
