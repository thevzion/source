# A world where anyone, a person alone, an association, a small team, can rely on the structure of an organization.

Intelligence runs what can be run. Only people want.

**Organizations of one, connected.** An organization is made of organizations of one, plugged into
it by `serves` links: a person's goal serves the organization's goal. Nothing is taken from the
person; what is shared is shared by pointer. Your work is shared, not you.

Organizations link to each other, and organizations of one link directly too. The same structure
at every size, up to the scale of the planet.
