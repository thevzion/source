# Losing the thread

**Before.** You know you jump from idea to idea. Weeks later you notice that nothing you chose got
closed, and you can't say when it started.

**Imagine.** You wrote one question for yourself: every evening, it tells you whether you stayed on
what you chose, spread out, or drifted, with the facts (what moved, what it served, what you opened and
closed). It asks; it never decides. You set it up, and you can turn it off.

**Status.** Imagined, not seen yet.
