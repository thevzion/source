# Which roles can intelligence not hold, and for how long?

Every role can be held by anyone or anything. Some roles, today, only people hold: physical work,
care, presence. People hold them by choice, or for now, until machines can. Which ones stay with
people, and why, is open.

Settled by: what organizations observe, role by role.
