# What probability threshold does a decision model use, and who sets it?

A decision model answers a closed question on one object, with a probability, and hands over below
a threshold. Open: the threshold, who sets it, and which tasks it takes first.

Settled by: calibration on a sample labeled by the person or by a large AI model (testing the
tester).
